TABAJA CARD DESIGNER V8.1 — PRODUCTION EDITION

SMART PHOTO NAMING
1. Start every employee photo filename with the Employee ID from Excel.
2. You may write the employee name after the ID.

Accepted examples:
000001.jpg
000001 Sajed Tabaja.jpg
000001-Sajed Tabaja.png
000001_Updated Photo.webp

The application reads only the leading number 000001 for matching.
If two photos begin with the same ID, Pre-flight reports a duplicate and shows both filenames.
